## What Fomo asks, and when

1. **Account access, before wallet mechanics.** [VERIFIED DOCUMENTATION] Current help describes Apple or Google signup and app-created wallets. It does not ask users to create a seed phrase during signup. This is Fomo's documented model, not an independent custody audit. [Account creation](https://help.fomo.family/en/articles/14430434-creating-your-fomo-account) and [product overview](https://help.fomo.family/en/articles/15170883-what-is-fomo).

2. **Funding questions depend on the chosen method.** [VERIFIED DOCUMENTATION] The Deposit screen offers methods available to that account. Crypto deposits ask for the network and matching asset. Card funding asks for an amount and payment details. Direct Apple Pay token purchases differ from funding the cash balance. These are separate tasks, not one permanent account form. [Deposit guide](https://help.fomo.family/en/articles/14430450-how-to-deposit-funds) and [crypto deposit instructions](https://help.fomo.family/en/articles/14434419-depositing-with-crypto-usdc).

3. **Choose an asset before entering the trade.** [VERIFIED DOCUMENTATION] Home, search and token pages provide discovery and context. Account settings live separately in the profile. Network routing for trading uses a shared cash balance, according to Fomo's help. Deposits still require the correct network. [Navigation](https://help.fomo.family/en/articles/14430486-navigating-the-app), [token browsing](https://help.fomo.family/en/articles/14436535-browsing-tokens-on-fomo) and [supported chains](https://help.fomo.family/en/articles/14436558-supported-chains).

4. **One amount, with shortcuts.** [VERIFIED PUBLISHED IMAGE] The official desktop image shows a selected asset beside an order panel. That panel has Buy/Sell, one dollar amount, quick amount buttons, available cash, a fee and a token-specific Buy button. The pictured shortcuts are `$25`, `$50`, `$100` and `$250`. These are screenshot controls, not recommended Falcon budgets or verified live quotes. [Desktop product image](https://fomo.family/images/landing/fomo-desktop.webp).

5. **Reveal consequences before confirmation.** [VERIFIED DOCUMENTATION] Fomo describes an estimated quote before confirmation. Network fees appear in the quote. Significant price impact affects the estimate. A large network-fee share prompts an additional confirmation. Trade history and position details provide later feedback. [Fee and quote help](https://help.fomo.family/en/articles/14436214-trading-fees-on-fomo) and [transaction history](https://help.fomo.family/en/articles/14436332-transaction-history).

## Why Falcon R3 and R4 missed the problem

[VERIFIED SOURCE] Falcon's current form exposes a reserve identifier and eight required numeric mandate fields together. A separate action form exposes source and target identifiers plus base-unit amounts. Source: `web/dashboard/Cfo.jsx:10-24,57-134,976-1042`.

[INFERRED] R3 reorganized configuration into steps. R4 made two configuration values larger. Neither changed the main task from configuring an engine to taking an investment action. A slider is not a substitute for choosing the right question.

[INFERRED CLASSIFICATION] Separate these responsibilities before drawing another screen:

| Responsibility | Current fields | Consumer treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Immediate intent | Action, chosen investment or holding, amount | Ask in the selected asset or position context. |
| Persistent owner capital policy | Reserve floor, per-action cap, target weights | Approve once, save, and expose an explicit edit path. |
| Persistent execution-risk policy | Position, slippage and fee caps | Keep the owner's approved limits. A preset requires explicit approval, not a hidden value. |
| Freshness and operations | Evidence age, monitor interval and cycle count | Show meaningful status. Moving numeric ownership to service policy requires a contract revision. |
| API representation | Asset IDs, base units, BPS, request IDs and hashes | Resolve from verified data or convert exactly. Keep audit access without making these primary inputs. |

[VERIFIED SOURCE] The current backend requires every numeric mandate value. Hiding a control cannot make its value optional. Source: `mesh/cfo/policy.mjs:18-21,138-180`.

[VERIFIED SOURCE] Some questions already have authoritative answers. The saved reserve determines the buy source and redeem destination. Saved mandates remove repeat setup. Invest/reinvest supports an explicit saved-allocation sizing choice; exits require an explicit size. Source: `web/dashboard/Cfo.jsx:263-280,424-433`; `mesh/cfo/policy.mjs:507-553`.

## Recommended consumer interaction

[INFERRED RECOMMENDATION] Start with actual cash and positions, then an action. Open a focused amount sheet for that action. Offer exact amount entry and an explicit saved-allocation choice where supported. Do not begin with reserve, fee, freshness and monitoring configuration.

[INFERRED RECOMMENDATION] Before signing, show the service-returned plan, review outcome, exact debit, minimum output, quoted costs and expiry. Show other economics only when verified data exists. Keep raw evidence in accessible details. Retain separate owner signing, relay approval and reconciliation. A missing or unknown result remains blocked.

[INFERRED RECOMMENDATION] Keep first-time policy approval separate from daily actions. Reuse approved settings for returning owners. Do not repeat those questions for each investment.

[RESOLVED DESIGN APPROACH] The owner selected the consumer policy-template model. The numeric approval is recorded below; adoption and consumer architecture remain subject to R5 approval. No capital amount, target investment or risk limit may be silently invented or activated.

[VERIFIED AUTHORITY LIMIT] This research does not authorize copying Fomo's wallet creation, embedded signing, funding rails or execution model. Falcon still requires owner approval for each financial action. No application files, backend contracts, deployments or financial actions changed during this research.

## Selected direction and approved guardrail values

[VERIFIED USER DECISION] The owner selected “Consumer policy template” through the structured choice. This selects a design approach only. It does not approve numeric limits, code changes, policy activation or spending.

[APPROVED VALUES, NOT ACTIVE] The owner replied “go ahead” after the exact draft table. This supersedes the earlier pending-value status. The listed preset values are approved for the template. They are not Fomo defaults or validated investment recommendations. Capital amounts, assets and weights remain explicit owner choices.

| Control | Approved template values |
| --- | --- |
| Capital boundaries | Owner chooses the protected USDC amount and maximum USDC per move. No monetary defaults. |
| Position concentration | At most 25% per receipt position, measured against the supported, quote-valued Intents portfolio. |
| Execution costs | Maximum slippage 0.5%; maximum conservative quoted roundtrip cost 1%. |
| Evidence freshness | Portfolio and signed quote evidence at most 5 minutes old; quote deadlines still apply. |
| Monitoring | Off initially. An explicit start permits 5-minute intervals, at most 12 read-only cycles. No model calls, signatures or relay. |

[PROPOSED AUTHORITY] Asset permissions and allocation weights remain explicit owner choices from verified service data. No protocol, target allocation, principal amount or return is invented. Daily use then asks for the selected action and amount, with an explicit saved-allocation option only where configured.

[VERIFIED APPROVAL BOUNDARY] The go-ahead approves the preset values, not policy activation or spending. A source-cited R5 architecture must still define adoption, version handling and exact input conversion before code changes. Current mandate validation remains authoritative: `mesh/cfo/policy.mjs:138-180`.

[INFERRED RISKS] A 25% cap is a proposed concentration limit, not a validated portfolio recommendation. A quoted cost cap does not insure principal. If a route fails the limits, the service must block it rather than loosen the template. Provider terms, retention, T82, paid review and exact funded-trial gates remain separate.
