Presenter notes · simulation

[VERIFIED, deterministic local simulation] The model uses available = max(0, cash − due − reserve). A $1M allocation passes the base case. It is blocked by $400K when due rises by $400K, by $500K when cash falls by $500K, and by $250K when reserve rises by $250K. The test reads this slide’s script in test/falcon-investor-deck.test.ts. These synthetic scenarios test reserve protection. They do not prove yield or investment performance.
