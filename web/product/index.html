The product / 01

A firm view for agent decisions.

FalconOS gives agents a shared evidence record, specialist advice, and an independent risk gate. The client agent keeps the final decision.

Firm model

Advice with a visible boundary.

Falcon Investment is a council of specialist advisory agents. It does not custody funds, sign transactions, or submit orders.
